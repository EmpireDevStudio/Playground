Android Battery Optimization Tip

Android may pause background browsers after 10–30 minutes unless set to Unrestricted:

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Chrome (or 50-Pt Studio)
  2. Tap Battery → select Unrestricted
  3. In Notifications, enable Show on lock screen
